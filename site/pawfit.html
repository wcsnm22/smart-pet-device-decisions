Pawfit review, price, plan and discount — FAQ

How much does Pawfit cost?

The Pawfit 3 tracker is £54.99 plus a subscription: Basic from £3.99/month (billed £95.76 every 2 years) up to £9.99/month monthly, or Premium from £3.99/month (billed £143.64 every 3 years) up to £6.99/month billed yearly.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27

What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

Both include live location updates every 5 seconds, unlimited range, 24-hour history, activity monitoring, safety zones and family sharing. Premium raises the single tracked-walk duration from 2 hours to 6 hours and lists more consistent location update rates during walk tracking.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27

Can I get my money back?

Pawfit publishes a 30-day money-back guarantee, an 18-month warranty and free delivery on the Pawfit 3 product page.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27

How often does the tracker update location?

Pawfit states live location tracking updates every 5 seconds, with a Bluetooth-based Find Nearby mode for pinpoint accuracy indoors.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27

Does Pawfit work for cats?

Yes - Pawfit's FAQ says Pawfit 3 is a lightweight tracker that can be used for cats, and the site sells a separate cat tracker range; the same FAQ notes the tracker uses 4G, so adequate network coverage is needed.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27

How many pets can I track on one account?

Pawfit's app feature list says you can track up to 20 pets at once, with family sharing so several people can look after the same dog.

Source: https://www.pawfit.com/en-gb/product/pawfit-3.html · checked 2026-09-27
