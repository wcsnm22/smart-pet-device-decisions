What the answer comes down to

If you want the lowest published price, PETKIT's catalog floors at $159.99 for the gray Crystal Duo (a limited-time deal) and $199.99 for the camera model, both against $299.99 - and the Max 3 sits at $399.99 against $499.99. Meowant's SC02 matches that $399.99 against $559.99, and CatGenie's Whoosh starts at $469.00.

If you want to keep your plumbing out of it, Litter-Robot, PETKIT and Meowant all store waste in a bin you empty yourself - only CatGenie asks for cold water and a laundry drainpipe or toilet, and then sells you its own litter system to go with it.

If your cat is heavy, the ceilings differ: Litter-Robot 5 and Meowant's SC09 publish 30 lb, PETKIT's boxes and Meowant's SC02 stop at 22 lb, and CatGenie publishes no weight limit but sizes its advice at one-three cats up to 20 lbs inside a 19.25 in opening.

On the try-it terms, Litter-Robot and CatGenie both print 90 days but refund different things (purchase price less shipping vs merchandise cost only), Meowant splits 90 days on the SC09 and 30 on the SC02, and PETKIT allows 30 calendar days from delivery with you paying return shipping. Read the linked returns page before you order, not after - every one of them puts conditions on the refund.
