Tractive review, price, plan and discount — FAQ

Does Tractive need a monthly subscription?

Yes. Tractive states that 'the subscription price is separate from the tracker' and lists plans from 13 €/month monthly down to 5 €/month on the 2-Year Basic plan (120 € billed every 2 years).

Source: https://tractive.com/en/c/plans · checked 2026-09-27

How much is the Tractive tracker itself?

The DOG 6 is listed at 69 €, the DOG 6 XL at 79 € and the DOG 6 XL Adventure at 99 € on the tractive.com/en store on the check date; Tractive notes pricing may vary by location.

Source: https://tractive.com/en/pd/gps-tracker-dog · checked 2026-09-27

What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

Both include live GPS tracking, heart and respiratory rate monitoring and activity/sleep monitoring. Premium adds family sharing, 365-day location history and GPS data export; Basic gives 24-hour location history.

Source: https://tractive.com/en/c/plans · checked 2026-09-27

How long does the battery last?

Tractive publishes up to 2 weeks for DOG 6, up to 6 weeks for DOG 6 XL and DOG 6 XL Adventure, and up to 7 days for the collar-integrated CAT 6 Mini.

Source: https://tractive.com/en/pd/gps-tracker-dog · checked 2026-09-27

Can I get my money back?

Tractive publishes a 30-day money-back guarantee on its plans page, and free shipping on all trackers.

Source: https://tractive.com/en/c/plans · checked 2026-09-27

Is device protection available?

Tractive sells an optional Tractive Care add-on for loss, damage or theft at 2 €/month on top of a 1-Year plan (24 € annually) or 1,50 €/month on a 2-Year plan (36 €).

Source: https://tractive.com/en/c/plans · checked 2026-09-27
