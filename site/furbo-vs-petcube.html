What the answer comes down to

If price decides it, Petcube's menu floors at $26.99 for the Petcube Cam (was $34.99) and its 360° model is $34.99 (was $52.99); Furbo does not do tiers - the dog and the cat camera are the same $54 against 'original price is $184', and the default button on the page is 'Subscribe now - $137.92' because the yearly plan is pre-selected.

If the subscription decides it, Furbo sells two tiers across three billing periods (six published rates, $6.49 to $12.49 per month as printed) with a 3-day history on Standard, while Petcube keeps a free Basic tier, puts 3 days on Optimal and 90 days plus 24/7 Online Vet chat on Premium - and separately sells a camera bundle 'starting from $5.99/mo'. Both give you 14 free days first.

If the paperwork decides it, Petcube's 45 calendar days beat Furbo's 30 on returns, but Furbo prints 'Free US Shipping' where Petcube prints no threshold, and each brand dangles a longer warranty only while you stay subscribed (Furbo) or move to Premium (Petcube). Read the condition with the number.

If the room decides it, Furbo's dog camera rotates 360° with treat tossing built into both models, Petcube spreads the same money across a pan-tilt Cam 360, a treat-dispensing Bites 2 Lite and a cheap fixed Cam - and only Petcube also sells a GPS tracker, which is the one device in either catalog that cannot work without a paid plan.

Which is cheaper, Furbo or Petcube?

The cheapest camera each store prints on 2026-09-30: Petcube's Petcube Cam at $26.99 against $34.99, and Furbo's 360° dog and cat cameras at $54 against 'original price is $184'. Do I have to pay for a subscription with either brand?

No, on both sides.
