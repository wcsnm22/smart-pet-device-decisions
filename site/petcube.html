Petcube review, price, plan and discount — FAQ

How much do Petcube devices cost?

On the check date Petcube listed Cam at $26.99 (was $34.99), Cam 360 at $34.99 (was $52.99), Play 2 at $64.99 (was $99.00), Bites 2 Lite at $79.99 (was $149.99) and the GPS Tracker at $29.99 (was $52.99).

Source: https://petcube.com/ · checked 2026-09-27

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No. Petcube's FAQ states there is no monthly fee by default; a Petcube Care cloud-recording plan is optional and costs $9.99/month on the tier advertised with Bites 2 Lite.

Source: https://petcube.com/cam-360/ · checked 2026-09-27

What is Petcube's return policy?

Petcube publishes a 45-day hassle-free money-back policy for devices purchased through its own store, Petcube.com.

Source: https://petcube.com/cam-360/ · checked 2026-09-27

What warranty do I get?

One year from the date of retail purchase in the US and Canada, two years in the EU and UK, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use; Petcube may repair, exchange or refund at its discretion.

Source: https://petcube.com/docs/warranty/ · checked 2026-09-27

What is the difference between Petcube Cam and Cam 360?

Cam 360 rotates 350° horizontally and 55° vertically while Cam does not; other listed features are the same, including 1080p HD video, 2-way audio and 8x digital zoom. Neither includes a laser pointer - that is on the Play 2.

Source: https://petcube.com/cam-360/ · checked 2026-09-27

Does the Petcube GPS Tracker need a data plan?

Petcube sells the tracker as a $29.99 device with 'unlimited LTE/4G connectivity' and up to 30 days of battery in power save mode; the checked pages publish no separate monthly tracker fee.

Source: https://petcube.com/tracker/ · checked 2026-09-27
