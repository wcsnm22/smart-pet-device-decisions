Litter-Robot review, price, plan and discount — FAQ

How much does a Litter-Robot cost?

Litter-Robot publishes 'As low as' prices of $699 for Litter-Robot 4, $799 for Litter-Robot 5 and $899 for Litter-Robot 5 Pro, and shows financing from about $44 per month.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/litter-robot-4.html · checked 2026-09-27

Can I return a Litter-Robot if my cat won't use it?

Yes - Whisker publishes a 90-day in-home trial: return within 90 days for a full refund, paying return shipping. Returned units must be clean, and a cleaning fee applies if they come back with litter or waste.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/money-back-guarantee.html · checked 2026-09-27

What warranty is included?

1 year of WhiskerCare coverage is included with every unit; a 3-year WhiskerCare plan costs $129 on Litter-Robot 4 and $149 on Litter-Robot 5, covers the robot top-to-bottom with no deductibles and includes free shipping both ways.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/litter-robot-4.html · checked 2026-09-27

Does Litter-Robot require special litter?

Whisker recommends a standard-weight clumping clay litter such as GreatLitter. Non-clumping, loose-clumping, newspaper-based and wood pellet litters typically do not work because they do not filter properly through the sifting system.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/faq.html · checked 2026-09-27

Can I pay over time?

Whisker offers 0% APR financing through Affirm on eligible purchases with 3, 6, 12 or 18-month plans, alongside credit cards, Shop Pay, Amazon Pay and PayPal.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/payment-options.html · checked 2026-09-27

Is Litter-Robot HSA/FSA eligible?

Litter-Robot's product page states HSA/FSA eligibility; eligibility is determined per customer through Flex after a brief telehealth consultation, approved within 24 hours, and varies by employer plan.

Source: https://www.litter-robot.com/litter-robot-4.html · checked 2026-09-27
