Furbo review, price, plan and discount — FAQ

How much does a Furbo camera cost?

Both the Furbo 360° Dog Camera and the Furbo 360° Cat Camera are listed at $54, each showing 'original price is $184' on the US store on the check date.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-dog-camera · checked 2026-09-27

Do I have to pay for Furbo Nanny?

Furbo presents Furbo Nanny as a plan you choose at purchase: Standard starts at $9.99/mo monthly or an average of $6.99/mo yearly, Premium at $12.49/mo monthly or $8.74/mo yearly, with 2-year options at $6.49/mo and $8.12/mo for the first two years respectively. The Standard tier includes a 3-day video history.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-dog-camera · checked 2026-09-27

Is there a free trial?

Furbo advertises 'Get a FREE camera + 30 days of Furbo Nanny' for first-time customers: take a camera home with Furbo Nanny included and just pay shipping.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-dog-camera · checked 2026-09-27

What is the return policy and warranty?

Furbo publishes a 1 Year Warranty, free US shipping and free returns within 30 days on its US product pages.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-dog-camera · checked 2026-09-27

Are there any official discounts?

The US product page banners '$10 off your first Furbo' when you sign up with e-mail, and every purchase includes the Furbo For Good donation of $1 per Furbo. No other public promo code is published on the checked page.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-dog-camera · checked 2026-09-27

Does the dog camera differ from the cat camera?

Furbo lists them as separate models - Furbo 360° Dog Camera and Furbo 360° Cat Camera (the cat model adds a feather wand toy) - at the same $54 price with the same 'original price is $184' and the same Furbo Nanny plans.

Source: https://furbo.com/us/products/furbo-360-cat-camera · checked 2026-09-27
