What the answer comes down to

If you want the lowest published price, PETKIT's catalog floors at $199.99 for the Purobot Crystal Duo and $399.99 for the Max 3 (regular $499.99), and Meowant's SC02 sits at $399.99 against $559.99. Both are app-controlled boxes with no plumbing printed on the page.

If you want no plumbing at all, Litter-Robot plugs into the wall and empties into a drawer: the FAQ says a Litter-Robot 5 drawer may need emptying once every 10 days in a one-cat household, against once every 8 days for Litter-Robot 4, and it takes clumping clay litter only.

If you never want to touch waste, CatGenie is the only one of the four that flushes - it wants cold water and a laundry drainpipe or toilet, and it sells you its own litter system, either disposable Whoosh litter or reusable washable granules with a $25.99 SaniSolution cartridge.

On the try-it terms, Litter-Robot and CatGenie both print 90 days, Meowant prints 90 days on the SC09 and 30 days on the SC02, and PETKIT's policy allows 30 calendar days from delivery. Every one of them puts conditions on the refund - return shipping, a return authorization, a 20% handling charge or a restocking rule - so read the linked returns page before you order, not after.

What litter can I use in a Litter-Robot?
