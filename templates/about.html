About

Pet Smart Device Decisions is a small static site that answers smart pet device questions - prices, subscriptions, trial windows, warranties and promos for products like Litter-Robot automatic litter boxes, Furbo and Petcube pet cameras, and Tractive and Pawfit GPS trackers - with facts taken only from each brand's own official website and official product, help or policy pages.

How it works:

  • Every fact on this site carries the official source page URL it was read from and the date it was checked.
  • Nothing is estimated, rounded, or filled in from memory. If a figure is not published on the brand's official site, it is not repeated here.
  • Promotions are reported only when the brand's own site publishes them. Where no official promo page exists, the page says so.
  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or compensated by any brand listed on this site. Product labels, feeding guides and your veterinarian's advice always govern.
